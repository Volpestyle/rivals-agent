## The one product loop

A learned policy acts in the live practice range, and gets scored there. Work that doesn't feed that loop
waits. Offline gates (self-fed replay, teacher-forced skill) remain diagnostics; they don't replace live evidence,
because replay never shows the policy the consequences of its own actions.

## Two tracks for experiments (James's decision)

- **Explore.** Fast offline sweeps on the full admitted 180-minute train cohort and frozen dev. No pre-registration,
  no hash pinning, no judge. Output: a one-page result in the lane note, tagged EXPLORATORY (dev is heavily
  reused). Nothing exploratory reaches a real fit or a pilot without a confirm run. Validation, test and sealed data
  stay closed. Compute: the Mac (free) while round 3 runs on Modal; any cloud spend for explore is a separate budget
  that James sets, not round 3's $50.
- **Confirm.** The present discipline (pre-registration, independent judge, pins) for any candidate that crosses
  the explore bar, before it's called a result or goes near a pilot.
- Required independent review is unchanged where AGENTS.md mandates it: code that sends live input, and code that
  decides what enters a training or evaluation set.
