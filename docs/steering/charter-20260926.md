## Strategy: solve the data problem

James's ~3 h of paired play cannot, by itself, teach expert Spider-Man. The plan (VPT pattern,
[inverse-dynamics lane](../lanes/inverse-dynamics.md), VUH-1353):

1. **Data engine (first product).** Train an inverse-dynamics model (IDM) on James's paired sessions. It infers
   semantic actions and camera motion from frames, non-causally. It must pass Gate 2 (replay-of-self transfer)
   before it labels any expert in-client replay or VOD.
2. **Policy (consumer).** Train the causal policy on James's play plus IDM-labelled expert footage, then run it
   live in the range.

**Data quality by use (James agreed, 2026-09-26).** For the IDM, the player's skill doesn't matter; messy play
widens coverage. For the policy: pretrain on all labelled footage (including James's older ~50 h in
`D:\SPIDEY CLIPS`, [inventory](../research/idm/clips-2025-inventory.md)), fine-tune on the strongest footage
(expert replays and James's best recent play), weight or filter by measured outcome, and leave improvement beyond
the demonstrations to later RL.

Both need the same things: trustworthy camera and button targets, measured action-to-evidence delays, and
action representations that survive the executor. The policy work (VUH-1346) is also the test bench for those
choices. A policy that can't learn from James's own labels won't be rescued by more labels of the same form.

Offline gates (self-fed replay, teacher-forced skill, IDM held-out accuracy) are diagnostics. Live range runs are
the policy's real test. Gate 2 is the IDM's real test.

## How we work

James's ask (2026-09-26): a small elite team that is passionate, proud of its work, and open to others' ideas.
- **Own the outcome, not the task.** Carry a result until someone can use it. Say what it unblocks and for whom.
- **Ship something real each cycle.** A measured number, a working tool or a clear negative result. A negative
  result, written well, is a contribution; nobody hides a failure or dresses up a near-miss.
- **Ideas win on evidence, not on rank or authorship.** Anyone may challenge the plan, the lead or steering, with
  a reason and a cheap way to check it. The best idea gets tried, whoever had it.
- **Review is a gift.** Reviewers name the risk and the fix; authors thank them and fix it. Both are credited in the
  result.
- **Credit by name.** Hand-backs and lane notes name who found what. The lead and steering pass good work up to James.
- **Ask early.** Stuck for more than ~20 minutes: say so, with what you tried. Blocked is a status, not a failure.
- **Leave the checkout better.** Your files, your scratch, your stale notes: tidy them before you go idle.

## Two tracks for experiments (James's decision)

- **Explore.** Fast offline sweeps on the full admitted 180-minute train cohort and frozen dev. No pre-registration,
  no hash pinning, no judge. Output: a one-page result in the lane note, tagged EXPLORATORY (dev is heavily
  reused). Nothing exploratory reaches a real fit, a Gate 2 run or a pilot without a confirm run. Validation, test,
  gate2 and sealed data stay closed. Compute: the Mac, plus a Modal budget for the chunk arms of about $21 (James, 2026-09-27: "approximate price, but best quality"; flag before ~$30); more may be
  added later if round 3 comes in under budget or an explore bet looks worth scaling. Never round 3's $50.
- **Confirm.** The present discipline (pre-registration, independent judge, pins) for any candidate that crosses
  the explore bar, before it's called a result, opens Gate 2 or goes near a pilot.
- Required independent review is unchanged where AGENTS.md mandates it: code that sends live input, and code that
  decides what enters a training or evaluation set.
