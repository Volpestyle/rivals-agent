## 1. What alignment actually needs

The controlling criteria are [inverse-dynamics, “Gate 2 protocol” and its median-offset amendment](inverse-dynamics.md#gate-2-protocol-replay-of-self-2026-09-23), [the anchor consistency amendment](idm-gate2-anchors.md#lead-decision-2026-09-26-anchors-check-consistency-and-their-offset-is-a-measured-effect), and [the cooldown rejection](idm-gate2-anchors-results.md#re-validation-result-2026-09-26-fails-a3-replay-side-cooldown-anchors-are-out-of-gate-2).

The required result is a map between live and replay composition timestamps that leaves camera lag and smoothing measurable:

1. Split the replay into verified continuous, target-POV, 1× playback spans. Exclude pauses, seeks, follow changes, menus, overlays and unknown states. Logger operations are exclusion evidence, never expert inputs.
2. Match the same displayed timer events by round/phase and value. Repeated round clocks and repeated seeks cannot be associated by value alone. Fit a slope-1 offset from timer anchors only; report the free-slope check and reject `|slope - 1| > 0.001`.
3. Split timer sequences at extensions, resets and MM:SS/SS.d changes. Retain the registered minimum of 30 timer anchors per span; the T3' reader test requires at least three anchors per segment. Compare segment median offsets within one recorded frame. Report raw per-tick residuals rather than rejecting display jitter itself. The wording about support per segment versus per span should be made explicit in the new registration, not interpreted after scoring.
4. Check matched kill-feed shifts around their **own** per-span median offset from the timer fit. Report that median as an effect; reject residuals beyond two recorded frames. Do not shift the timer fit to make kills, actions or camera predictions agree. Cooldown checks were removed by the later decision.

At the recorded 120 fps, one/two frames are 8.33/16.67 ms; the model's 60 Hz interval is 16.67 ms. Preserve that distinction in scorers and reports.

**Timer alone is mathematically sufficient to estimate an offset, but is not sufficient under the accepted Gate 2 protocol.** A timer fit can be internally consistent while matching the wrong round, selecting a static team time-bank, or missing a playback discontinuity. The remaining non-timer channel tests a different HUD event stream. `replay_cuts` supplies exclusions, not corresponding timestamps, and cannot replace it. Timer-only would require a new design decision and its own validation; this plan does not silently waive the check.

The independent check also has limits: both HUD channels can share server/render delay. It tests consistency, not an independently known absolute physical clock. Report alignment uncertainty separately from model error; never call the residual camera lag a pure network measurement.

**Proposed clarification for the lead's registration:** require at least three uniquely paired, judged shifted arrivals in an accepted span, distributed over its usable duration, rather than letting zero/one event pass a vacuous consistency test. Exclude and count insufficient spans. This is a new conservative support proposal, not an existing accepted threshold. Test feasibility only on development pairs before fixing it; if coverage is inadequate, report that constraint rather than switching to timer-only during evaluation.
