## Target-bearing branch: information before output complexity

The [probe](target-bearing/analyse.py) tests bearing of the detection **nearest the crosshair**, not nearest in world distance and not necessarily James's intended target. Treat a consistent cross-session association and incremental prediction beyond recent human motion as grounds for **one input ablation**, not a fitted aiming controller. Its in-sample regression and row-permutation diagnostics do not establish held-session generalization or account fully for temporal dependence. Weak/inconsistent results or poor detection coverage leave the default branches unchanged.

Use only the current frame's bearing, validity and detector confidence/geometry; missing target is unknown, not centered. Preserve native-frame coordinates and inspect detection errors before extraction. Future turns are probe outcomes, never model inputs. A positive result advances bearing **ahead of chunks/camera-head complexity in rows 2/4**, but **after the registered real-fit draft in row 1**, after button repair in row 3, and after validity/skill repair in rows 0/5. It neither retrofits round 3 nor authorizes a live aim rule.
