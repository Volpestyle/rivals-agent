**The research direction is justified; several interpretations need restraint.**

The VPT precedent supports learning an inverse model from paired recordings, using it to label additional video, then training an acting policy. Its Minecraft work used roughly 2,000 paired hours and 70,000 hours for policy pretraining. That validates a strategy, not a minimum Rivals data requirement or a promise that three hours will suffice. [Primary research summary](https://openai.com/index/vpt/).

The latest charter correctly keeps the policy as a consumer and test of the data engine. I would preserve both outcomes: a small usable player and trustworthy additional labels. Making every playable milestone wait for a universal replay labeller would concentrate too much risk in the IDM. Camera, visible ability events and movement can become useful at different times; uncertain channels should remain masked. A camera-only transfer success would not establish reliable button labels. Ultimately, added inferred labels must improve the downstream policy against a paired-data-only control, not just expand the dataset. [Current charter](../steering/charter-20260926.md).

Three interpretation limits affect spending decisions:

1. **Self-fed recorded-video evaluation is a diagnostic.** `predict_self` uses the model's previous decoded actions while continuing to load the human recording's images. It cannot reveal the new states caused by the agent's own movement. The existing notes acknowledge this; headline summaries should too. Human-history persistence is also a useful diagnostic reference, not an autonomous competitor with the same information. [Implementation](../../policy/range_bc/train.py), [decision tree](round3-decision-tree.md).
2. **The zero-feature ablation does not prove that vision is the only bottleneck.** The old recipe showed little useful dependence on its visual features; the new encoders improve that diagnostic without resolving self-fed collapse. Zeroed features are outside the training distribution; temporal alignment, visual resolution, representation, action history, loss and decoding can interact. The new heads also have more parameters and omit pixel augmentation, so their comparison with the incumbent does not isolate pretraining. A matched previous-action-input ablation is a justified next question; it does not require another encoder sweep. [Explicit experiment caveat](../lanes/explore-policy.md).
3. **The NitroGen comparison tests its frozen visual tower.** It retains this project's recurrent policy and action heads; this negative control result does not reject the complete NitroGen architecture. Conversely, NitroGen's published results do not prove real-time Rivals control: its evaluation pauses games during inference, and its authors acknowledge limited long-horizon planning. Measure local capture-to-action latency before promoting any candidate. [Current code](../../policy/range_bc/explore_encoder.py), [NitroGen paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.02427v1).
